I am a robotics R&D engineer with an MSc in Applied Mathematics and a BSc in Robotics, focused on robot dynamics, control,
numerical simulation, and mathematical modelling of physical systems. I work across geometric mechanics, simulation, and
model-based and learning-based control, with hands-on experience building robotic systems.

I did my MSc in Applied Mathematics at the University of Twente, the Netherlands, with a thesis on the
[geometric structure of Cosserat elasticity](https://purl.utwente.nl/essays/108602) supervised by [Frederic P. Schuller](https://people.utwente.nl/f.p.schuller).
